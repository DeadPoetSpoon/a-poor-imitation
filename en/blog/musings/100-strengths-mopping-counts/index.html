Foreword

During my week off, after sorting through my letters from a pen pal, I went out and tried plenty of things I had never experienced before: a string quartet, stand-up comedy, an exotic-pet cafe, interactive theater... In the end I found that these really aren't my interests, and that I had been doing the things I love all along: books, movies, games. Sorting letters, watching films, and beating DOOM: The Dark Ages were my happiest moments of the break. Perhaps people always manage to find the life that suits them. This vacation felt like a piece of advice: "Now that you've tried these things, no more regrets — from here on, focus on what you truly love!" Anyway, time to begin — time to begin those unfinished things!
