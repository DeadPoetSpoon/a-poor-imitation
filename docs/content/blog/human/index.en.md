# "Human"

All thinking must start from a subject, and the subject of the API is undoubtedly the "Human." Everything will be composed of "Humans" or parts of "Humans." The breakdown of the "Human" will be the focus of this article.

# Brain

The core of the "Human" is the brain — the primary operating mechanism. It is divided into the Behavioral Brain, Perceptual Brain, and Decision-making Brain (names are tentative).

## Behavioral Brain

The Behavioral Brain handles all basic physical behaviors, both controllable and uncontrollable, such as breathing, movement, etc.

## Perceptual Brain

The Perceptual Brain handles all high-level abstract perceptions, both controllable and uncontrollable, such as hearing, memory, etc.

## Decision-making Brain

The Decision-making Brain analyzes information and determines all controllable behaviors, such as deciding on a destination.
